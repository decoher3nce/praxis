# Reading Bank: Books by Dimension

*An optional bank of books a coach can draw on in Phase 4 (Build the path). It supplements each dimension's Learning path; it does not replace it. Templates only: this file lists books, never people.*

Each dimension file in `04-dimensions/` already names one reading, one talk, and one other modality. That is enough to run a cycle. This bank is for the student who wants to go deeper, or the coach who wants a second option to offer.

---

## How to use it

1. **Readiness first.** Run the relevance and bandwidth check (`03-readiness-and-timing.md`) before any book comes up. A book does not make a dimension ready.
2. **One section, not one book.** Phase 4 says curate, don't data-dump. Offer a chapter or a single tool as the Input step. For a student with thin bandwidth, the dimension's own article or talk is the better input.
3. **The student picks.** Offer two or three inputs and let them choose. A manager handing over a book on hard conversations unasked reads as a flag, not a gift.
4. **Mind the borrowing trail.** If books come from a shared shelf with a visible sign-out, the title someone borrows can reveal the dimension they are working on. Keep borrowing unlogged, or keep coach copies for cycle use.
5. **Graduation feeds the shelf.** A student who reaches a confirmed strength can leave a one-page insert in the book: what they tried on real work and what happened. That is a Custodianship act (dimension 18, step 4). An insert is the author's own account and names no one else.

---

## Reading the tables

- **Fit.** *Direct* means the book addresses the dimension's behavioral indicators. *Partial* means it offers one useful tool or lens.
- **Fit grades are judgment calls**, made from each book's thesis against the indicators, not from a chapter-by-chapter check. Disagree freely and edit.
- **Section pointers are marked `[verify]`.** They were written from memory of the books. Chapter names shift between editions; confirm against the copy in hand before putting one in a path.
- **Named** marks a book the dimension file or the coach guide already cites.

## Adding to the bank

Follow the source convention in `04-dimensions/_index.md`: name the specific title and author, mark anything you have not confirmed with `[verify]`, and never fabricate a title, author, or URL. Keep each dimension to a handful of books. A long list defeats the purpose.
